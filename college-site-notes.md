# Seshadripuram College website notes

Source: https://www.spmcollege.ac.in/

Observed identity: Seshadripuram College, Seshadripuram, Bengaluru-20; permanently affiliated to Dr. Manmohan Singh Bengaluru City University. The site emphasizes NAAC A++, ISO 9001:2015, 50 years, “Always Aiming High,” and empowering/inspiring students.

Observed visual direction: dark navy/charcoal backgrounds in major hero and achievement sections; gold/yellow used for rankings, highlights, accent graphics, and calls to action; white content areas and white typography; circular “S” emblem/logo; institutional, formal, achievement-oriented photography; red/orange promotional badges appear in admission banners. Treat exact colors as approximate until extracted from the official logo/assets; use the official logo rather than recreating it.

Observed information architecture: top utility/accreditation links; navigation around About Us, Academics, Admission, Departments, Facilities, Students Corner, IQAC; content clusters for UG/PG programs, latest news and notifications, events, photo/video gallery, placements, question bank, admission process, syllabus/curriculum, library, placement cell, contact, and alumni.

UI implication: create a mobile-first app with a navy foundation, gold emphasis, white cards/surfaces, restrained red/orange status accents, strong academic hierarchy, and futuristic elements such as glass panels, subtle gradients, glowing gold focus states, charts, and clean data cards. Do not make it neon-heavy or reduce readability/accessibility.
